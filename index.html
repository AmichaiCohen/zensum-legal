Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play ZenSum?

Fill each row and column with the digits 1 to 5 without repeating any digit in the same row or column. Numbers inside each outlined cage must combine to match the cage’s target value using the shown operation (+, −, ×, or ÷).

Does ZenSum work offline?

Yes. All puzzles, difficulty levels, the Daily Challenge, and automatic progress saving work 100% offline. Optional rewarded video hints require an internet connection unless you unlock Remove Ads, which makes hints instant and offline-ready.

How do I restore my “Remove Ads” purchase on a new device?

Tap the Remove Ads pill in the top-right utility bar, then tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my game data?

ZenSum has no user accounts and stores your puzzle progress, scores, streaks, and preferences strictly locally on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.
