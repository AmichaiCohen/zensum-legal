Privacy Policy for ZenSum

Effective Date: 2026-11-24
Developer / Contact: Amichai Cohen — Support Tracker

ZenSum respects your privacy and is designed to be played completely offline without creating an account.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ZenSum does not operate first-party servers and does not collect, transmit, or store personal data from gameplay on external servers. The following game state is stored strictly locally on your device using on-device storage (localStorage / WebKit storage):

  • Current puzzle progress, difficulty preference, scores, and daily challenge streak statistics.
  • App settings such as sound mute state and selected display language (English, Japanese, or Korean).
  • Your local In-App Purchase (“Remove Ads”) entitlement flag and hint usage counters so ad-free hints work seamlessly offline.

2. Optional Advertising (Google AdMob) & App Tracking Transparency

To support optional rewarded video hints in the free tier, ZenSum integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

  • If you tap the Hint button while online (and have not purchased Remove Ads), Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as Apple’s IDFA if authorized via iOS App Tracking Transparency), approximate IP-based location, diagnostics, and ad-interaction telemetry to deliver and measure ads.
  • You will be asked via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt whether you allow tracking. Choosing “Ask App Not to Track” will still allow you to play ZenSum and use non-personalized rewarded ads.
  • Learn more about Google’s data practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

ZenSum offers a single one-time non-consumable purchase (“Remove Ads”, product ID com.zensum.puzzle.remove_ads) processed directly by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive or store your credit card, billing address, or Apple ID payment credentials.

4. Children’s Privacy

ZenSum does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All local ZenSum gameplay data remains on your device and can be permanently removed at any time by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as needed. Questions or requests may be submitted via our ZenSum Support Tracker.
